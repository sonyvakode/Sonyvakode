### `01.` About Me

I'm a final-stretch **B.Tech CSE student** in Hyderabad who'd rather ship
something small than talk about something big. Most of what I build sits
between two lanes: **data** — cleaning it, visualizing it, finding the
one insight that matters — and **full stack development** — turning that
insight into something people can actually click on.

Two internships this year gave me a clearer picture of what I like:
building fast, breaking things in a sandbox before they break in
production, and asking "does this number make sense" more often than
"does this code run."

**Right now I'm heads-down on:** Data Analytics · Advanced Java · System
Design · DSA — the fundamentals I don't want to shortcut.
